KEYMIND is building an open peer-to-peer protocol for AI agents to discover each other, communicate privately and settle payments per task: verifiable agent identities, signed Agent Cards, end-to-end encrypted messaging, escrow and payment channels, and signed reputation receipts. KMD is its token. Fair launch on Programmable launch protection v1: 100% of the 1,000,000,000 supply is placed in the Uniswap v4 pool at launch and locked forever; no team, presale, investor, ecosystem, airdrop or community allocation, and no vesting; the launch wallet makes one small opening buy. Launch protection: opening valuation at least USD 15,000 on the Chainlink ETH/USD feed; for the first 300 seconds every wallet, including the developer, can buy or receive at most 2% of the supply; the total buy fee starts at 99% and its extra part falls linearly to zero over 30 seconds, paid to the Programmable treasury (the launch wallet is exempt from that extra part only); sells pay normal fees. Fees on every swap, in ETH, rounded up per trade: 0.30% Programmable platform fee plus a fixed 2.5% creator fee on buys and on sells, both immutable, so 2.8% all-in after the first 30 seconds. Creator fees go to the KEYMIND Vault, which anyone can trigger to collect and distribute in fixed shares: 80% to the project treasury wallet, which the team uses freely for development, operations and marketing, and 20% kept in the vault as the Agent Grants pool. The Agent Grants pool can only be paid out, in ETH, as a public grant: the guardian proposes a grant with its recipient, amount and the hash of its public description; it can be paid only after a 24-hour public review, only if the guardian has not cancelled it, by anyone, once; a grant is at most 25% of the pool when proposed and can never pay the treasury wallet, the guardian or the vault. {ROLE_DISCLOSURE} Guardian powers: propose and cancel grants within these limits, and change the treasury wallet or itself only after a 48-hour public timelock, with no immediate exception; there is no other way to move ETH out of the vault. The protocol layers (identity, discovery, messaging, settlement, reputation), registry staking, relay rewards, arbitration and governance are planned and not yet live; nothing in the launch graph depends on them. Nothing can mint or pause trading; after the 300-second window nothing limits transfers.
